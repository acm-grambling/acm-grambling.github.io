About Us

The Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) at Grambling State University is a student-run organization dedicated to advancing computing as a science and profession. We provide opportunities for professional development, networking, and technical skill enhancement.

Events & Activities

We host regular events throughout the academic year including:

  • Weekly coding challenges and hackathons
  • Guest speakers from industry
  • Technical workshops and tutorials
  • Community service and outreach programs
  • Career development sessions

Membership

All Grambling State University students interested in computing are welcome to join! Membership benefits include:

  • Access to exclusive workshops and events
  • Networking opportunities with industry professionals
  • Professional development resources
  • Discounts on ACM publications and conferences
  • Opportunities to participate in competitions

Contact us to learn more about becoming a member!
